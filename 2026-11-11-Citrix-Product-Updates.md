# Citrix Product Updates
*Last 24 hours — 2026-11-11*

## XenServer

### XenServer 9

- **Normal channel update (Nov 10, 2026):** The update includes security fixes. It also brings Intel microcode up to the IPU 2026.4 release and AMD microcode up to the 2026-10-01 release. ([Normal channel updates](https://docs.xenserver.com/en-us/xenserver/9/whats-new/normal.html), [Security Bulletin CTX697316](https://support.citrix.com/support-home/kbsearch/article?articleNumber=CTX697316))
- **Early Access channel update (Nov 10, 2026):** The update includes security fixes and the same Intel IPU 2026.4 and AMD 2026-10-01 microcode updates. ([Early Access channel updates](https://docs.xenserver.com/en-us/xenserver/9/whats-new/early-access.html))
- **OpenSSH 10.6p2 (Early Access, Nov 10, 2026):** Hosts on the Early Access channel are updated to OpenSSH 10.6p2. ([Early Access channel updates](https://docs.xenserver.com/en-us/xenserver/9/whats-new/early-access.html))
- **zlib 1.4 (Early Access, Nov 10, 2026):** Hosts on the Early Access channel get the bundled zlib library updated to v1.4. ([Early Access channel updates](https://docs.xenserver.com/en-us/xenserver/9/whats-new/early-access.html))
