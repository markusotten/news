### Press

**Citrix launches AI-powered Session Insights.** A new capability for Citrix SecurAccess with Chrome Enterprise that records and analyzes browser sessions with AI to surface risky behavior, and — notably — distinguishes actions taken directly by an employee from those executed by an AI assistant or autonomous agent on their behalf, giving security teams evidence for agent accountability and recommendations to refine policy. (2026-09-15, [announcement](https://www.citrix.com/news/announcements/sep-2026/citrix-session-insights-launches-to-strengthen-security-for-user-and-autonomous-agent-activity-with-ai-powered-security-intelligence.html), also covered on the [blog](https://www.citrix.com/blogs/2026/09/15/introducing-ai-powered-session-insights/))

**Citrix modernizes its certification program.** Co-President Sridhar Mullapudi announced a shift away from fixed, product-centered certification levels toward role-based credentials for administrators and architects, emphasizing practical, scenario-based assessment of real-world operational responsibilities over product-configuration trivia; specific new certification paths are promised "in the coming months" but not yet named. (2026-09-15, [blog](https://www.citrix.com/blogs/2026/09/15/citrix-certifications-built-for-the-work-you-do/), also covered on [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fcNHbavso7E))

### Community

**Beyond DLP: custom browser behaviors worth detecting.** A Tech Zone blog post arguing that traditional browser security tooling (DLP, CASB, identity, SWG) sees isolated events rather than the full sequence of user activity, and showing how the new Session Insights AI prompt template lets teams define custom "deep inspection" behaviors — sensitive data sources, risky destinations, application categories — worth flagging beyond what off-the-shelf policies catch. (2026-09-15, [article](https://community.citrix.com/techzone-blogs/secure-private-access/secure-private-accessbeyond-dlp-custom-browser-behaviors-worth-detecting/))
